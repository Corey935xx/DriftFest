Good to know

The details.

A few quick answers about the DriftFest website concept.

Is DriftFest a real event?

No. DriftFest is a fictional festival concept. There is no real event, ticket sale or booking.

Where is the festival set?

The concept is set at NEC Birmingham. This website is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NEC.

Can I buy a ticket?

No real tickets are on sale. The prices and ticket options are illustrative only; the buttons demonstrate a simple selection interaction.

Are the photographs from DriftFest?

No. The photographs are genuine motorsport images by independent photographers, used as visual references for this fictional concept. They do not show a DriftFest event.
